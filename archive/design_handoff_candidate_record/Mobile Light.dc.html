Four screens for a recruiter away from the desk

Five things a recruiter does away from the desk, in the order they happen: find a person or client fast; call, text or email them from the record; log the call before the next one; see the candidate as the client will (CV, snapshot, pay, availability, tags, who owns them); and put an interview in the diary. Nothing else. Briefs, commercials, compliance, consent and adverts stay on the desktop.

Read and react, not administer. The phone carries Needs you, next actions, quick logging, and the moves that cannot wait: advance, book, note. Everything else says "on the desktop". Same ink, bone, sand and berry; a bottom bar instead of the sidebar; one primary action per screen. Weight sits at the top: the thing that needs you, then the people, then the lists, each capped at three with an "All" link so a screen fits in one and a half thumbs of scroll.
